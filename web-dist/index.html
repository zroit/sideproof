A fair test, on half your face

Dermatologists test products with a split-face trial. SideProof gives anyone the same design with a phone.

  1. Sleep, weather and light change your whole face

    So a before-and-after photo can't tell you what the product did.

  2. The product goes on one half

    The app picks the half at random. The other half gets nothing: it is the control.

  3. YouCam Skin AI maps both halves

    Each scan returns a map for every concern. SideProof splits it at your face's midline and measures each half.

  4. Only a change past the noise counts

    Two photos before you start measure your face's own difference. The product has to beat it 2.5 times.
